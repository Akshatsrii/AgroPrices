# 🌾 AgroPrice AI — Phase 0: Product Vision & Market Research

## 1. Product Vision
**AgroPrice AI** is an intelligent, farmer-centric agricultural price forecasting and trade decision platform designed to eliminate market information asymmetry for Indian farmers. By leveraging real-time Mandi market trends, historical price volatility, transportation dynamics, and AI-driven predictive intelligence, AgroPrice AI empowers farmers to choose **when**, **where**, and **at what price** to sell their crops to maximize net profit.

### Core Mission Statement
> *"To maximize every farmer's profit per harvest by delivering actionable AI trade recommendations, transparent Mandi price comparisons, and instant buyer offer evaluations directly to their mobile screens in their regional languages."*

---

## 2. Market Opportunity & Landscape
- **Target Audience**: 140+ Million agricultural landholders across India (Smallholder, Medium commercial, and FPO managers).
- **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**: $350B Indian Agricultural Output Value.
- **Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)**: $45B digital agtech & post-harvest market intelligence sector.
- **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**: $1.2B tech-enabled direct crop trading & mandi analytics.

## 4. Key Value Propositions
1. **Zero Guesswork Selling**: AI Decision Score (0-100) tells farmers whether to sell today or hold for 3-7 days based on dynamic arrivals and historical price patterns.
2. **True Net Profit Transparency**: Computes exact profit after deducting fuel, toll, labor, and mandi commission charges.
3. **Smart Offer Evaluator**: Evaluates trader bids instantly against fair market values and suggests exact counter-offer prices.
4. **Hyper-Local Mandi Radar**: Visual maps showing distance, prices, and arrival volumes for nearby mandis within 100 km.
