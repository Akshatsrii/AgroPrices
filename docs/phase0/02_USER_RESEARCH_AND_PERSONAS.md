# 🌾 AgroPrice AI — Phase 0: User Research & Personas

## 1. Primary Farmer Personas

### Persona 1: Ramesh Kumar (Smallholder Farmer)
- **Age**: 42 | **Location**: Sehore, Madhya Pradesh
- **Landholding**: 3.5 Acres | **Primary Crops**: Wheat, Soybeans
- **Tech Literacy**: Smartphone user (WhatsApp, YouTube in Hindi)
- **Pain Points**:
  - Relies on local middleman (Arhtiya) for price quotes.
  - Lacks vehicle to haul produce to distant mandis without confirmed high prices.
  - Urgently needs cash post-harvest to pay off seed/fertilizer loans.
- **AgroPrice AI Solution**:
  - Visual 8-step crop listing with instant AI profit forecast.
  - Trader offer evaluator showing if local middleman bid is fair or unfair.

---

### Persona 2: Balvinder Singh (Medium Commercial Farmer)
- **Age**: 38 | **Location**: Karnal, Haryana
- **Landholding**: 18 Acres | **Primary Crops**: Basmati Rice, Mustard, Potato
- **Tech Literacy**: Tech-savvy, uses online banking and digital Mandi portals.
- **Pain Points**:
  - High logistics cost when driving tractor trolley 40 km to a Mandi only to find glut prices.
  - Wants 7-day price forecasting to store grain in local warehouse or sell immediately.
- **AgroPrice AI Solution**:
  - Multi-Mandi side-by-side net profit comparison (Price minus fuel/labor).
  - "Sell vs Wait" Decision Engine with storage cost dynamic modeling.

---

### Persona 3: Sunita Devi (FPO Lead & Aggregator)
- **Age**: 34 | **Location**: Nashik, Maharashtra
- **Landholding**: Manages 120 Farmer Producer Organization (FPO) members | **Crops**: Onion, Tomato
- **Tech Literacy**: Advanced web & app user.
- **Pain Points**:
  - Managing bulk pricing and market volatility for multi-ton shipments across state borders.
  - Negotiating transparent transport contracts.
- **AgroPrice AI Solution**:
  - Admin dashboard, bulk price history comparison, and negotiation helper assistant.
